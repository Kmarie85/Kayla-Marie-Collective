The Problem (Before)

I didn’t need “a pretty site.” I needed a platform that works like a filter: it should attract aligned founders and quietly repel the wrong-fit inquiries. The risk with most services sites is that they sound broad, vague, and interchangeable — which leads to low-quality leads and constant back-and-forth.

The Goal

Build a credible services platform that communicates: who I help, what outcomes I deliver, and exactly how to start — while staying lightweight, fast, and easy to maintain.

The Build (What I Implemented)

  • Outcome-first service copy to reduce decision friction
  • Conversion path designed around one clear next step: “Start a Project”
  • Custom semantic HTML + CSS (no builder bloat)
  • Mobile-first layout for readable, calm scanning
  • SEO foundations: metadata, canonical URLs, clean structure
  • GA4 tracking wired to CTAs so I can see what converts

Results (After)

  • Clear positioning for web + digital product builds
  • Stronger qualification: the copy sets expectations up front
  • A real conversion asset (not just a gallery page)
  • Measurement in place: I can attribute clicks and inquiries to specific pages

Why This Matters (For Clients)

This is the same build philosophy I bring to client work: start with clarity, design for decision-making, and track what people actually do. A site should do more than “look good” — it should move the right people forward.
