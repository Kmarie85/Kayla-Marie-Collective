The Objective

The goal was to build a credible services platform — not just a portfolio. This site needed to communicate who I help, what I do, and how to start, while filtering out misaligned inquiries and demonstrating real technical execution.

The Approach

  • Defined a clear service structure with outcome-driven copy
  • Built from scratch using semantic HTML and custom CSS
  • Made mobile-first layout decisions for readable flow
  • Used conversion-focused CTAs (“Start a Project”) to guide qualified leads
  • Added SEO foundations: metadata, canonical URLs, clean structure
  • Kept the build lightweight for speed and maintainability

The Result

  • A polished services brand that reads professional and grounded
  • Clear positioning for web and digital product clients
  • A portfolio asset that demonstrates both creative and technical skill
  • A foundation designed to scale with future case studies

Why This Matters

Kayla Marie Collective was built as an internal studio project to define standards and systems before applying them to client work — the same approach I bring to every build.
