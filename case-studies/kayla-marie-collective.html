Before (What Wasn’t Working)

  • “Pretty” isn’t the problem. A services site can look good and still fail to convert.
  • Broad positioning attracts broad leads. Vague copy invites the wrong inquiries and creates endless back-and-forth.
  • No clear decision path. When every link is equal, people hesitate instead of taking the next step.
  • Unmeasured behavior becomes guesswork. If CTAs aren’t tracked, you don’t know what’s working.

The Goal

Build a credible studio site that communicates who I help, what outcomes I deliver, and exactly how to start — while staying lightweight, fast, and easy to maintain.

Constraints I Built Around

  • Speed matters: founders browse quickly, mostly on mobile.
  • Clarity beats clever: service offerings needed to be instantly understandable.
  • Simple upkeep: the site had to stay easy to update without builder lock-in.
  • Track the real actions: CTAs needed proper GA4 events so decisions aren’t based on vibes.

What I Changed (The Approach)

  • Wrote outcome-led service copy to reduce decision friction and qualify inquiries.
  • Designed a single conversion path around one primary action: “Start a Project.”
  • Built with semantic HTML + custom CSS to avoid builder bloat and keep performance strong.
  • Focused on mobile-first readability so the site feels calm, scannable, and credible.
  • Installed SEO foundations (clean structure, metadata, canonical URLs) for long-term discoverability.
  • Wired GA4 conversion tracking to key clicks so the site can be improved based on data.

After (What Improved)

  • Clear positioning for web + digital product builds without sounding generic.
  • Better lead quality because expectations are explicit from the start.
  • A real conversion asset (not just a portfolio gallery).
  • Tracking in place so future updates are informed by behavior, not guesses.

Who This Build Is For

  • Founders who want a site that feels calm and premium — without fluff
  • Service providers who need clear inquiries and less back-and-forth
  • Business owners ready to stop “having a website” and start using it to support revenue
