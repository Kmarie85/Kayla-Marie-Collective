The Problem (Before)

The business was relying on an outdated WordPress site that didn’t reflect the quality of their work. It was harder to control structure, performance, and tracking — and the overall experience didn’t feel as professional as the craftsmanship being delivered in real life.

The Goal

Create a site that’s fast, clean, and easy to maintain — with solid SEO foundations and real tracking in place so the business can measure growth and inbound actions over time.

The Build (What Was Implemented)

  • Rebuild from scratch using semantic HTML/CSS/JS (no page builders)
  • Migration + deployment via GitHub Pages
  • Domain + DNS setup + propagation troubleshooting
  • SEO foundations: metadata, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs
  • Analytics: GA4 installed in the <head> correctly
  • Event tracking for key actions (calls + form submissions)
  • Mobile UX: spacing, nav behavior, and gallery structure improvements

Results (After)

  • A site that matches the brand: clean, modern, craftsmanship-forward
  • Full ownership: no template lock-in or plugin maintenance
  • Clear navigation and mobile-friendly browsing
  • Measurement installed so future improvements are data-driven

Why This Approach

This build prioritized long-term ownership and performance over convenience. Instead of stacking plugins, the site is structured to scale cleanly — without breaking every time an update rolls through.
