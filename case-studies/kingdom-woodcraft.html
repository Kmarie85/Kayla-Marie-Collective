Client Project

From outdated WordPress to a fast, trackable site that supports quote-ready leads

Kingdom Woodcraft needed a site that matched their craftsmanship: clean structure, quick load, clear navigation, and measurable actions (calls + inquiries).

  • Performance: lightweight custom build for faster load
  • Clarity: cleaner structure and navigation on mobile
  • Measurement: GA4 + conversion events installed correctly
View Live Site Start a Foundation Website

Before (What Wasn’t Working)

  • The site didn’t match the work. The craftsmanship was premium — the website looked dated.
  • Mobile browsing was clunky. People couldn’t quickly scan services and decide what to do next.
  • No clean measurement. The business had no reliable way to track calls, inquiries, or what pages mattered.
  • WordPress upkeep was friction. Plugins and templates created maintenance stress and performance drag.

The Goal

Build a fast, clean website that feels as professional as the work — and makes it easy for quote-ready customers to take the next step (call or inquire), with tracking installed correctly.

Constraints We Worked Within

  • Small business reality: the site needed to work without constant upkeep.
  • Speed matters: customers browse fast, especially on mobile.
  • Clear actions: calls + inquiries had to be obvious and measurable.

What I Changed (The Approach)

  • Rebuilt the site for performance with a lightweight custom structure (no builder bloat).
  • Reorganized content for decision-making so visitors can scan, trust, and take action quickly.
  • Handled domain + deployment correctly (DNS + propagation) to avoid “site is down” chaos.
  • Installed analytics + conversion events so calls and inquiries can be measured over time.

After (What Improved)

  • Cleaner, modern presentation that matches the quality of the business.
  • Better mobile experience for fast browsing and easier navigation.
  • Ownership + stability without plugin maintenance or template lock-in.
  • Tracking in place so future changes are based on real behavior, not guesses.

Who This Build Is For

  • Trades, makers, and local service businesses who need a site that looks legitimate fast
  • Owners who want simple maintenance and clear inquiries
  • Businesses ready to stop “having a website” and start using it to support leads
