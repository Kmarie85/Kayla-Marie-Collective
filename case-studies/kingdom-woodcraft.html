The challenge

The craftsmanship was premium— the website wasn’t. Mobile browsing felt clunky, WordPress upkeep was friction, and there was no clean measurement for calls or inquiries.

Outdated presentation

The site didn’t match the quality of the work, which weakens trust.

Mobile friction

People couldn’t quickly scan services and decide what to do next.

No measurement

Without tracking, you can’t tell what’s working or what to improve.

The approach

Rebuild for speed and ownership, simplify the decision path, and install tracking on the actions that matter—calls and inquiries.

  • Custom rebuild: faster load, fewer moving parts
  • Clean structure: navigation and content organized for quick scanning
  • Launch handled correctly: domain + DNS + deployment done without downtime stress
  • Analytics wired: GA4 + conversion events installed and verified

The outcome

A modern, stable site that loads quickly, reads cleanly on mobile, and supports quote-ready leads—with tracking in place so updates are based on behavior, not guesses.

  • Cleaner presentation that matches the work
  • Better mobile experience for fast browsing
  • Ownership + stability without plugin maintenance
  • Tracking in place for calls and inquiries
