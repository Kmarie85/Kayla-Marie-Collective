Referral Overview

A quiet overview you’re welcome to share.

What I do

I design and build calm, clear, conversion-friendly websites for small and wellness-focused businesses that want their online presence to feel aligned, professional, and easy to navigate — not overwhelming or salesy.

I focus on clarity, structure, and user experience so visitors can quickly understand what a business offers and take the next step with confidence.

Who I’m a great fit for

  • Wellness practitioners (massage, yoga, therapy, coaching)
  • Local shops and service businesses
  • Small teams or solo founders
  • Businesses outgrowing “Facebook-only” or DIY sites
  • People who value clarity over hype

Common reasons people reach out

  • Their site feels scattered or outdated
  • Customers can’t easily find services, hours, or booking
  • They rely too heavily on social media for basic information
  • Their online presence doesn’t match the quality of their work

How projects usually start

  • A short clarity call to assess fit
  • Sometimes a small audit or cleanup
  • Sometimes a full site build or refresh

Most projects begin with clarity, not commitment.

Why people refer to me

  • Calm, professional approach
  • Clear communication
  • Thoughtful structure and UX
  • Reliable follow-through

Referrals

If you know a business owner who might benefit from a clearer, more grounded online presence, I’d be grateful for an introduction. I’m always happy to return referrals when it’s a good fit.
