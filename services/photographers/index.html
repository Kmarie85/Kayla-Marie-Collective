The booking flow

What a photographer website should do

1

Show your best work first

Curate. Lead with the work you want more of — not everything you’ve ever shot.

2

Make packages easy to understand

Clear starting points + what’s included. No walls of text. No confusion.

3

Filter inquiries (politely)

A form that asks the right questions so you get fewer “just wondering…” messages.

4

Move people to book

Strong CTAs + the next step spelled out (availability, turnaround, how it works).

If your current site doesn’t do these things, you’re not behind — you just need a cleaner structure.

FAQ

Do you work with all types of photographers?

Yes. Weddings, families, couples, seniors, brand photography, and more. The build is structured around your session types and your booking flow — not a one-size template.

Will my galleries load fast?

Yes — performance is part of the build. We’ll keep layouts clean, optimize images appropriately, and avoid heavy bloat so mobile browsing stays smooth.

Can you help me organize my portfolio?

Absolutely. We’ll pick a structure that’s easy for clients: by session type, featured stories, or a curated “best of” approach — whatever fits how you want to be booked.

Do you integrate with HoneyBook / Pixieset / Studio Ninja?

We can connect your site to the tools you already use. The goal is a clean handoff from “I love your work” to “here’s the next step.”

What if I don’t have perfect copy?

You don’t need perfect. I’ll guide structure and provide prompts so you can write faster — and we’ll keep wording clean, confident, and aligned with your style.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — larger projects can be split into payments so you don’t have to wait. All payments are completed before launch.
