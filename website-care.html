What It Is

Support after launch, without handing everything back to you

Website Care is an optional monthly support service for Kayla Marie Collective clients who want continued help after their site goes live.

It is designed for business owners who do not want to troubleshoot small issues, make routine edits themselves, or worry about who to contact when something needs attention.

Your website build is still a one-time project. Website Care simply gives you an ongoing support option if you want it.

A good fit if…

  • You want your site to stay current
  • You do not want to make edits yourself
  • You want support from someone who already knows your site
  • You would rather send a request than figure it out alone

What It Helps With

The small things that still matter

Website Care is built for light, ongoing support - the kind of updates that keep your site accurate, polished, and functioning without turning into a full redesign.

Content Updates

Text edits, image swaps, button updates, link changes, and small content refreshes across your existing pages.

Light Layout Adjustments

Small spacing fixes, simple formatting cleanup, and minor visual adjustments that help your site stay tidy and user-friendly.

Technical Support

Domain connection help, DNS troubleshooting, basic site monitoring, and support when something small needs attention.

What’s Included

Ongoing support, with clear boundaries

  • Hosting and technical configuration support
  • Domain connection and DNS troubleshooting
  • Minor content updates
  • Small layout or spacing adjustments
  • Basic website monitoring
  • Email support for site-related questions

Typical turnaround is 3–5 business days depending on scope and workload, with faster support available on the Priority plan when possible.

What’s Not Included

Not meant for major rebuilds

  • Full redesigns or rebrands
  • New pages or major new sections
  • Advanced integrations or custom development
  • Marketing strategy, ads, funnels, or copywriting
  • Large revision batches or daily requests

If you need larger updates or new functionality, that work is quoted separately before anything moves forward.

FAQ

A few common questions

Website Care is meant to keep support simple and clear. These are the questions that tend to come up most often.

Do I need Website Care to keep my site live?

No. Website Care is completely optional. Your website build is still a one-time project, and your site can remain live without an ongoing monthly care plan.

What counts as a small update?

Small updates typically include things like text edits, image swaps, button or link changes, minor formatting adjustments, and simple content refreshes on existing pages.

Larger changes like new pages, major section redesigns, rebrands, or advanced functionality are quoted separately.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. If your support needs change, you can move between the Standard and Priority plans based on what makes the most sense for your business at that time.

What if I need something outside my care plan?

If a request falls outside the scope of Website Care, I will let you know before moving forward. Larger updates, new features, or more involved work are always quoted separately and only completed with approval.

Is Website Care available for any website?

Website Care is primarily intended for Kayla Marie Collective clients. If your site was not built by KMC but you are interested in ongoing support, reach out and I can let you know whether it is a fit.
