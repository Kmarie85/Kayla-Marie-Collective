Case Study

Lead With Jim

Helping a faith-driven creator bring coaching, content, podcasting, and leadership under one clear digital home.

View Live Site

The Challenge

Years of meaningful work needed one clear place to live.

Jim had years of coaching, speaking, podcasting, writing, and leadership experience. The problem was not a lack of substance. The problem was that visitors could not quickly understand the full depth of what he offered or where to start.

His site needed to bring his coaching, content, podcast, newsletter, and resources together without making the experience feel cluttered or confusing.

The Strategy

We rebuilt the experience around clarity, trust, and next steps.

01

Clarified the path.

We organized the site around the main actions visitors needed: understand Jim’s work, explore coaching, listen to the podcast, read the newsletter, and reach out.

02

Connected the content.

The podcast, blog, newsletter, and resources were brought into one cohesive structure so visitors could move through his world without feeling scattered.

03

Built for trust.

The new design gave his brand a stronger first impression while keeping the tone grounded, human, and aligned with his leadership work.

The Outcome

A stronger online home for coaching, content, and connection.

The finished site gave Jim a professional digital home that made his work easier to understand and easier to navigate.

Clearer navigation

Visitors can quickly find coaching, podcast episodes, blog content, newsletter links, and contact information.

Stronger positioning

The site now better reflects Jim’s depth of experience and the direction his brand is moving.

Connected content ecosystem

Podcast, newsletter, blog, and coaching now support each other instead of living like separate pieces.
