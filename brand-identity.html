How it works

Breaking Down the Branding Process

Clear phases. Clean feedback points. You’ll always know what’s happening and what’s next.

Phase 1: Discovery

We start with an in-depth brand questionnaire + a direction check-in to understand your business, your audience, and what you want your brand to communicate.

This is where we lock in clarity before design begins — so we’re not guessing, and you’re not paying for “pretty” that doesn’t convert.

  • Brand questionnaire + goals
  • Audience + positioning alignment
  • Creative direction approval

Phase 2: Concept Development

I build 1–2 refined visual directions based on your strategy. You’ll review and choose one direction to move forward with — clean decision point, no endless options.

  • 1–2 polished directions (not 8 rough drafts)
  • Logo exploration + supporting marks
  • Initial color + type direction

Phase 3: Refinement

We refine the chosen direction with structured feedback. Your revisions are focused: we polish what’s working and tighten what’s not — without drifting into a new concept.

  • Up to 2 revision rounds included
  • Typography + palette finalized
  • Logo suite locked + balanced

Phase 4: Delivery

You receive final logo files (web + print formats), your brand colors + type system, and a simple brand guide so you can use everything correctly and consistently.

  • Organized file handoff
  • Brand guide PDF
  • Web-ready formats included
