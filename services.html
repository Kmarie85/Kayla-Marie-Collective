FAQ

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — larger projects can be split into payments so you don’t have to wait to move forward.

Can I update it myself?

Yes. You’ll get simple training so you can update text, images, and pages without a developer.

No problem. I’ll guide you with structure and placeholders so your launch doesn’t stall.
