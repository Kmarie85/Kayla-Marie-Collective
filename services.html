Questions Before We Start

A few things clients usually ask.

If you are wondering whether you are ready, you probably are. Most projects start with pieces, notes, and questions.

How long does a website project take?

Most website projects take 3–6 weeks depending on scope, content needs, revisions, and how quickly materials are provided.

Do I need to have all my content ready?

No. You can come with notes, an old site, rough ideas, or half-written copy. Part of the work is helping organize the message.

Do you only build websites?

No. I also help with workbooks, journals, guides, lead magnets, launch pages, and digital pieces that support your bigger offer.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. After launch, we can talk through updates, maintenance, and continued support if you want help keeping things moving.
