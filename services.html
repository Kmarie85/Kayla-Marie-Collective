FAQ

What does a website project typically cost?

Most projects land in a clear package range, with optional add-ons depending on what you need (extra pages, copy support, email list setup, etc.). If you tell me your goals and what you’re starting with, I’ll recommend the cleanest option.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — larger projects can be split into payments so you don’t have to wait to move forward. All payments are completed before launch.

What’s the typical timeline?

Most sites take 2–4 weeks once content and approvals are moving smoothly. Larger builds or projects with heavy copy + photo needs can take longer. I’ll give you a clear timeline up front.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short intake form, your logo/brand basics (if you have them), and any content you already have. If you’re not ready with copy or photos, I’ll guide you with structure and placeholders so the build can still move forward.

What if I don’t have copy or photos?

Totally fine. I can build the structure first using placeholder copy and image guidance, then help you swap in the final content before launch. Your site won’t stall just because you’re gathering materials.

Do you write the copy?

I can. Some clients bring copy, some want help clarifying their message. If you want copy support, we can add it on — or I can provide a simple outline and prompts so you can write faster.

Can I update it myself?

Yes — you’ll get simple training so you can update text, images, and pages. If you’d rather not touch it, I also offer optional website care.

What platforms do you build on?

Most builds are fast, clean static sites (GitHub Pages / Netlify) or simple platform builds, depending on what you need. I’ll recommend the best fit for speed, budget, and long-term ease.

Will my site work on mobile?

Yes — mobile responsiveness is built in from the start. Your site will be tested across common screen sizes so it feels clean and easy to use.

Do you include SEO?

I include foundational SEO: page titles, meta descriptions, clean structure, image alt text, and performance basics. Ongoing SEO strategy (blogs, backlinks, advanced keyword plans) can be added if you want to build traffic over time.

Will you connect my domain and hosting?

Yes. I can help connect your domain and set up hosting based on your build. You’ll own all accounts in your name, and I’ll document what’s set up so you’re not locked in.

Do I own my website when it’s done?

Yes — once final payment is complete, the site is yours. You’ll have access to your files and/or platform accounts and can take it wherever you want.

How many revisions are included?

You’ll have a clear review process with scheduled revision rounds so feedback stays simple and organized. The exact number depends on the package, and it will be outlined before we start.

Do you offer ongoing website care?

Yes — optional care is available for updates, seasonal swaps, small tweaks, and keeping things running smoothly. You can decide later; it’s not required to launch.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. If your site feels outdated, confusing, or hard to update, we can rebuild it into a cleaner, more modern version without losing what already works.

What if I need something custom?

If you have a specific feature in mind (booking, formś gallery, lead magnet funnel, etc.), tell me. I’ll either build it, recommend an add-on, or suggest a clean tool that fits your budget.

This isn’t about rushing to launch.

It’s about building something that feels right, works well, and supports where your business is actually going.
