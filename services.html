Services FAQ

Answers to common questions about scope, pages, timelines, and fit.

What exactly do you build?

I design and build websites, landing pages, and simple funnels. This includes structure, layout, visual design, and technical setup so your site is clear, functional, and easy to navigate.

I don’t offer templates or partial fixes — each build is intentional and aligned with your goals.

What do you mean by “page”?

A page refers to a web or landing page (for example: a sales page, opt-in page, thank-you page, or services page).

It does not refer to product content, workbook pages, course lessons, or PDFs.

Do you write the copy or create the product itself?

Not by default. Clients provide the product content and core messaging.

My role is focused on structure, clarity, layout, and conversion flow, with light copy polish where noted. Full copywriting or product creation is only included if scoped as an add-on.

What if I’m not sure which tier I need?

That’s normal. Most clients don’t come in knowing the exact tier.

After you reach out, I’ll help you choose the level that fits your goals and scope — no pressure to upsell.

Can you work with Gumroad, Beacons, or Stripe?

Yes. I regularly integrate checkout and delivery using tools like Gumroad, Beacons, Stripe links, ConvertKit, and MailerLite, depending on what fits your setup best.

Do projects include revisions?

Yes. Each project includes a defined revision window focused on clarity and alignment.

Revisions are not open-ended redesigns or scope changes.

What if I need more than what’s listed?

Additional pages, copywriting support, email capture, digital product integration, or photography can be added as optional add-ons.

Anything outside the listed scope is discussed and approved before work begins.

Is this a good fit if I want something fast or inexpensive?

Probably not. My work is best suited for clients who value clarity, intention, and long-term usability over speed or bargain pricing.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

I’ll review your project details and follow up with next steps or clarifying questions.

If it’s a good fit, I’ll outline scope, timeline, and pricing clearly before we move forward.
