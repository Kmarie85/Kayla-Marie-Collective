Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 1, 2026

Kayla Marie Collective, LLC (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Information you provide: We may collect personal information that you voluntarily submit through our website, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Business name (if provided)
  • Any details you include in your message or inquiry

Information collected automatically: We may collect limited, non-identifying information about how our site is used, such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • General device and browser information
  • Approximate location (city/region level)
  • Referring/exit pages

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Respond to inquiries and contact requests
  • Communicate about services you’ve asked about
  • Improve website performance, content, and user experience
  • Understand which pages and content are most useful

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Contact Forms (Formspree)

When you submit a form on our website, your information is processed and delivered using Formspree, a third-party form handling service. Form submissions are used only so we can receive and respond to your message.

Formspree may process the data you submit in order to provide their service (for example, to deliver form submissions to us and help prevent spam). We recommend reviewing Formspree’s privacy practices if you would like more detail.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand website traffic and usage patterns. GA4 may collect information such as pages visited, links clicked, time spent on the site, and general device information. This data is typically aggregated and used to improve site clarity and performance.

You can opt out of certain tracking by adjusting your browser settings, using privacy-focused browser tools, or blocking analytics scripts. (Some site functionality may be affected by strict blocking.)

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to operate our website and communications (such as hosting providers, analytics platforms, and form tools). These providers are given access only to the information necessary to perform their services.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request to:

  • Access the personal information we have about you
  • Correct or update your information
  • Request deletion of your information (where applicable)

To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
